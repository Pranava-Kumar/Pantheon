# Product Guidelines: Pantheon - Advanced Market Analysis & Trading Platform

## Prose Style
- **Direct & Authoritative:** Use a concise, confident tone that communicates high-stakes market information clearly and with professional authority.
- **Precision First:** Avoid conversational filler; focus on providing precise technical and financial data that users can act on immediately.

## UX Principles
- **Efficiency-Driven Design:** Prioritize the most critical data points and market signals to minimize user cognitive load and effort.
- **Progressive Disclosure:** Present high-level summaries by default, but allow experts to drill down into detailed technical analysis and raw data.
- **Data Integrity First:** Ensure all data, especially for scoring (MMCI) and trading, is accurate, verified, and presented with its source context.

## Branding Tone
- **Futuristic & Sleek Tech:** A modern, high-tech aesthetic tailored for sophisticated algorithmic traders and market professionals.
- **Modern Performance:** Communicate speed and reliability through clean, data-centric design and intuitive navigation.

## AI Interaction Guidelines
- **Action-Oriented AI Output:** Focus on delivering final AI-driven insights and clear, actionable recommendations.
- **Verified Recommendations:** While prioritizing action, ensure that AI-generated scores and trends are always supported by empirical market data.
